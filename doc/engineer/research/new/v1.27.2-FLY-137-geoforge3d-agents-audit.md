## Implications

### Dept ownership is already encoded (just not in filesystem)

The conceptual dept-grouping exists in three places:
1. `README.md` mapping table (per-row Linear label)
2. SQLite `agents.domain` CHECK constraint (`product | operations | marketing | ...`)
3. FLY-127 `DepartmentRegistry` (Bridge enforces Lead-to-issue dept scope at spawn time)

What's **missing**: physical filesystem grouping. Today all 11 .md files are flat; ownership is implicit-via-metadata.

### Cross-discipline within Product is real

`product-lead` (Peter) currently owns 8 executors. These cover multiple disciplines (backend, frontend, designer, qa, PM, plan-generator). Inside Product team this is a natural sub-hierarchy. Trade-off question: do we treat "product" as 1 dept folder (8 files inside) or further sub-group?

### Marketing is an open gap

`marketing-executor.md` exists but has no `marketing-lead` Lead. Options:
- Add marketing-lead now as part of v1.27.2 (scope creep into FLY-137?)
- Park marketing-executor as orphan until marketing-lead is filed separately
- Treat marketing as part of operations-lead temporarily

### `cos-lead` does NOT spawn Runners

Simba's role is triage + delegation. Lead identity rules say "cos-lead does NOT load department-lead-rules.md; does NOT spawn Runners directly; delegates approve/reject/retry/spawn to Peter/Oliver."

This means cos-lead has **no executors of its own** in the dept-grouped hierarchy. Whatever shape we pick, cos doesn't get an `agents/cos/` subdir.

### Generic vs general

- `general-executor.md` exists in GeoForge3D (project-specific catch-all)
- v1.27.0 introduced Flywheel-shipped `generic-executor.md` (true zero-config fallback)
- These are **two layers**: project-level catch-all (general) and Flywheel-level absolute-fallback (generic). v1.27.1 keeps both; this audit confirms they're distinct.

## Open questions (will brainstorm with Annie 1-3 per round)

1. **Filesystem grouping**: dept-hierarchy mirror of `.lead/` (`<project>/.flywheel/agents/{product,operations,marketing,shared}/`) vs flat-with-metadata (`<project>/.flywheel/agents/*.md` + dept field in config.yaml `agents:` block) vs hybrid?
2. **Marketing handling**: file marketing-executor under `marketing/` even though no marketing-lead exists yet, or defer it to a separate FLY issue alongside marketing-lead creation?
3. **Cross-discipline within Product**: keep 8 files flat under `product/`, or sub-group (`product/engineering/`, `product/design/`, `product/qa/`)?
4. **Shared / catch-all placement**: where does `general-executor.md` live in the hierarchy? `shared/general-executor.md`? `<project root>/.flywheel/agents/general-executor.md`?
5. **AgentDispatcher walk order**: if hierarchy is dept-grouped, dispatch lookup order? E.g. for a Runner spawned by product-lead with label `frontend`: walk `product/` first → fall back to `shared/` → fall back to Flywheel-shipped `generic-executor.md`?
6. **Config block representation**: nested (`agents.<dept>.<name>`) vs flat-with-field (`agents.<name>.department: "product"`)?
7. **Migration path**: how to `git mv` 11 flat files to dept dirs (per-executor mapping is in README mapping table — automatable).
8. **Dispatch — Lead-driven vs label-driven**: today FLY-127 enforces "Lead → 1 dept → matching label". With dept-grouped agents, should AgentDispatcher accept "owning dept" as an additional input, or stick with pure label matching?
