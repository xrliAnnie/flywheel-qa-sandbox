# Design Review — FLY-1251 plan.md (Round 3)
Date: 2026-07-14
Author: Codex
Status: CHANGES REQUESTED

## Summary

Round 3 closes most of the previously identified design gaps: nonterminal reconciliation is now state-oriented, stale observation is durable and unbounded, manual QA has a credible coordinator-owned admission path, and the six-route authority matrix is much clearer. The plan is still not safe to hand to implementation, however: ambiguous Discord sends and channel-health handling can still strand an untracked clickable message, the Contents API cannot establish the promised Git-mode/type authority, and the required FLY-1244 writer seam does not exist in the already-landed commit A and omits additional compile/semantic changes needed for the new hold reasons.

## What's Good (Keep)

- The delivery boundary remains honest: this issue closes the execution-local incident shape and execution-scoped card contract without claiming the deferred workflow-run/generation barrier.
- The exact E1 incident remains mechanically closed. `qa_required=0` is no longer a card-posting exemption, while docs-only is the sole server-computed exception.
- Wrapping the full `reviewHoldReason` store-read sequence in one fail-closed boundary is the right convergence point. Per-method throw tests are materially stronger than a single generic failure fixture.
- `manualSpawnQa` is now specified at the correct layer. Reusing the existing issue-owner/admission gates, atomically claiming the record, and permitting same-head revival only after a dead-runner check and a `stuck|failed` CAS gives legitimate code PRs an escape hatch without creating a caller-minted PASS path.
- The attempt model, partial live index, complete nonce-history scan, state-specific restart fixtures, and explicit intentional blockage while a `posting` row remains unresolved are all good safety choices.
- Treating `grey_edit_done` only as a frequency-tier switch and retaining observation until verified message absence now matches R9. Separate durable cursors for high- and low-frequency sets make the bounded scan fair and restart-safe.
- Revoking the unsafe four-route transition and requiring a six-route shared authority is correct. Text/voice redirection and converting deferred approval replay into notification-only semantics align with main channel A.
- The source-discriminated authority mutation matrix, E7 persisted-substate matrix, E10 low-frequency observation test, and expanded E5 concurrency/revival cases are the right adversarial test shapes.
- The plan continues to keep the four FLY-1244-owned files out of FLY-1251 and correctly defers the merge-gate truth-table change until the sibling claim work is ready.

## Issues & Recommendations

1. **[HIGH] Two paths still bypass nonce recovery, and visible `posting` cards are not in the reaction observation set.**
   - **Why it matters:** §4.2 step 2 retires the attempt on any POST API failure. In the current notifier, timeout/abort/network errors and 5xx responses are `transient_failed`; Discord may have committed the message before the client lost the response. Retiring immediately can therefore leave the accepted message visible with no stored ID. A second bypass appears after nonce-scan failures: §4.2b correctly says an unresolved `posting+NULL message_id` row must stay posting, but after three failures §4.5 marks the channel unhealthy and retires every live card. Because `posting` is in the live index, that generic transition can move the unresolved row to `retiring/retired` without ever recovering its ID. Finally, §4.4 scans only `retiring|retired`; a `posting` row whose ID has been recovered/persisted but whose binding is not active remains clickable and unobserved, despite E9 promising an explicit response for clicks on posting cards.
   - **Suggested fix:** Establish one invariant: `posting AND message_id IS NULL` may transition only after a complete nonce scan proves either “found” or “absent.” Ambiguous POST outcomes (timeout, connection loss, 5xx, malformed success body) stay posting and enter that reconcile path; only definitive pre-send/config/client failures may retire directly. Channel-down handling must leave unresolved posting rows in place and alert/retry until reconciliation is possible. Add `posting AND message_id IS NOT NULL` to the high-frequency non-authorizing reaction scan until it becomes active or retiring. Extend E7/E11 with an accepted-then-timeout POST, channel-down during incomplete nonce scan, and a reaction on `posting+ID`.

2. **[HIGH] The second-stage docs classifier still cannot prove the mode/type contract and misclassifies several Git entry transitions.**
   - **Why it matters:** `GET /contents/{path}` does not expose Git mode. It can also report a submodule as `type: "file"`, and a symlink targeting a normal repository file can be returned as the target’s content; GitHub documents both behaviors in the [Repository Contents API](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/repos/contents?apiVersion=2022-11-28#get-repository-content). Therefore `type === 'file'` is not authoritative for either gitlinks or symlinks, and it cannot distinguish `100644` from `100755`. The algorithm also skips all removed entries and checks only the head side of ordinary modifications, so removal of a docs-path gitlink/symlink or a symlink→file/mode-only transition can be granted the docs-only exemption. The DDL shown in §3.1 also still omits `base_oid`, even though the prose says the column exists.
   - **Suggested fix:** Use Git tree entries for both base and head. GitHub’s [Git Trees API](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/git/trees?apiVersion=2022-11-28#get-a-tree) supplies the authoritative modes (`100644`, `100755`, `120000`, `160000`, `040000`) and entry types. Check the base entry for removed/modified/renamed-old paths and the head entry for added/modified/renamed-new paths; any missing, truncated, non-regular, executable, or changed-mode result is ship-relevant unless the policy explicitly ratifies it. Require `truncated=false` or use a complete targeted tree walk. Add `base_oid TEXT NOT NULL` to the actual DDL and test removed symlink/gitlink, symlink→file, and `100644↔100755`, not only head-side symlink presence.

3. **[HIGH] The FLY-1244 dependency named by the plan is not the seam that exists in source, and the missing contract is wider than `cardAuthority`.**
   - **Why it matters:** FLY-1244 commit A is already present as `35a04f510`, but its shared writer has no `cardAuthority` hook and no route-source argument. Its `holdReasonFor` type accepts only `codex_pending|qa_not_green|merge_block`, and its disposition logic rejects only `merge_block` while deferring every other hold. Consequently, after adding `qa_evidence_missing|qa_evidence_unknown`, passing the real callback is a TypeScript type mismatch; widening it naively would then make the two explicitly NEVER-deferrable reasons deferrable. The six-route source matrix also cannot be enforced until every sibling-owned caller supplies a trusted source discriminator. Calling “commit A” the completed prerequisite lets an implementer incorrectly conclude that PR-2 is unblocked, while §11 says there are no open items.
   - **Suggested fix:** Define and land a distinct FLY-1244 seam follow-up commit before PR-2 merge, then pin its hash in this plan. That sibling commit must include: the source enum propagated by all six routes; the injectable hook and typed rejection result; a `ReviewHoldReason`-compatible type; and disposition mapping that uses the NEVER-deferrable classifier for the two new reasons. Add one cross-branch compile/integration fixture proving all six routes reach the hook and that `qa_evidence_missing|unknown` reject rather than defer. Until that commit exists, mark PR-2 authority work as blocked—not merely “commit A prerequisite met”—while PR-1 and non-authority PR-2 mechanics may proceed.

4. **[HIGH] An active card that becomes held can still consume a click silently on the current production route.**
   - **Why it matters:** The live FLY-1244 reaction handler checks `deps.isHeld` before invoking the shared writer; when held it audits and returns `null`. The proposed writer hook therefore never runs and no typed founder-visible reason is produced. §4.4 does not cover this case because the card is still active. This is the PRD’s explicit late-hold adversarial case: whether the card remains active or transitions to retiring, the click must write no approval and must receive a visible hold reason. The six-route table describes non-active and text behavior but does not specify removal/replacement of this existing silent precheck.
   - **Suggested fix:** Specify the production wiring change: remove the reaction handler’s silent held short-circuit or replace it with a typed blocked-response path, and ensure the shared authority/hold result is rendered in the founder thread. Define whether a late hold retires the card or leaves it active-but-blocked; either is valid, but active-held reactions must be polled and answered. Add E13 cases for an active card under each of `codex_pending`, `qa_not_green`, `qa_evidence_missing`, `qa_evidence_unknown`, and a store-read exception.

5. **[MED] Several stale statements now contradict the Round-3 design and would misdirect the separate implement session.**
   - **Why it matters:** Architecture decision #7 still says edit success determines whether observation may stop; §8 still says manual spawn reuses the private path; risk #3 says two authority forms exist; §11 resurrects the revoked transition form and says there are no open items; the snapshot DDL omits `base_oid`; and the send sequence lists the activation marker before the CAS it must follow. The function-level exception rule also says any error on a “reviewable role” returns the new reason, which includes `implement` and conflicts with the stated main-only scope/E12.
   - **Suggested fix:** Perform one consistency pass after resolving the items above: make CAS activation precede marker write, update all file/risk/open-item sections to the public coordinator and hard seam dependency, remove every reference to the revoked transition, add the missing schema column, and state the exception result by role (`main → qa_evidence_unknown`; preserve the intended implement behavior). Keep the title/claims consistent with the rule that R9 is not complete until the seam and six-route tests are green.
