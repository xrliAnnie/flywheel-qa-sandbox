Codex credential-home convergence is now a safe, repeatable operation instead of a manually hunted quiet window. The reconciler derives the approved roster from the two managed Flywheel runner homes and the registered Codex app-server Leads, writes durable current-digest attempt receipts, skips active or unprovable homes without touching their contents, backs up a regular `auth.json` before linking it to canonical truth, clears copy-pending markers, and records already-satisfied homes idempotently. Lead and runner launch paths share admission fences with the migration path, and bounded child-process teardown retains the fence whenever exit cannot be proven.

The operation runs only on existing rhythms: the updater, Bridge health rider, and controlled restart window. The health rider is fail-closed by default and is enabled only by the canonical production Bridge wrapper; test-deploy slots explicitly force it off, so an ambient or forwarded environment cannot reconcile production homes from unmerged code. No launchd job, plist, crontab, cron entry, interval scheduler, feature-flag writer, or activation wrapper was added. A one-day configurable overdue threshold emits the registered `codex_home_migration_overdue` engineering alert through the normal durable alert queue, including retry and destination-preservation behavior; the integration test uses a real local HTTP receiver and the mutation test proves a disabled overdue condition turns red. A generic guard also prevents policy, projects, roster, build, or state corruption from silently suppressing an existing overdue obligation: if no normal alert was produced, it emits a daily deduplicated warning explicitly labeled as an alert-pipeline fault, never passes a mention, and persists a local delivery receipt keyed by UTC day, layer, and reason. Only the actionable missing-receipt-over-N-days path remains severe.

Readiness now exposes two honest scopes. The original global checker remains authoritative for runtime admission and preserves unknown desktop readers. A separate registered-home checker requires the exact five-home roster, current inventory digest and build, durable satisfaction receipts, live symlink topology, no pending markers, collector completeness, and strong resident daemon binding evidence. Its immutable evidence is content-addressed. The read-only CLI prints both registered and global results and always reports `activation.authorized=false`; registered acceptance cannot substitute for global runtime readiness. FLY-2729 deployment/QA evidence is verified only through its immutable artifact, accepted `qa_passed` claim, land mapping, and deployment mapping, and may remain pending without being forged. Host-derived evidence root and deployed SHA bindings cannot be supplied or overridden by the external dependency JSON; an override attempt is rejected as `dependency_binding_override`.

The required fixture proofs are green: active homes produce `skipped` with zero target-home writes; idle homes back up, link, clear the marker, and produce `done`; repeat execution produces `already-satisfied` without another backup or home mutation. Missing receipts cross the threshold and really send an alert; registered readiness covers the five-home positive path plus missing/stale receipt, wrong build, pending marker, census failure, registered CommDB orphan, unapproved live home, desktop unknown, resident identity/socket/PID mismatches, and forged or incomplete FLY-2729 chains.
