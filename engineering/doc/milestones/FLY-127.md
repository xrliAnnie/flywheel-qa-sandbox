- Updated the shared department Lead rules and doc-flow transport example so every spawn carries the current Lead identity and a department mismatch remains silent and undispatched.
- Left `CLAUDE.md` unchanged, as required by the implementation-node contract.

## Rollback and residual risk

- Setting `BRIDGE_DEPT_SCOPE_REJECT=off` restores legacy missing-identity auto-resolution and disables both the new identity-presence and department-scope rejection gates. This is a security regression by design and should be time-boxed to incident rollback only.
- The Bridge still authenticates callers with shared tokens, so `leadId` is asserted rather than cryptographically bound to a Lead. Per-Lead credentials are the durable follow-up.
- Existing stale-blocker handling and Linear hydration can occur before the new missing-identity response; this preserves the approved error-precedence contract but may finalize or alert on an otherwise rejected request.
- Standard QA injection now intentionally requires the target issue to carry the deployed slot's `deptLabel`; the current FLY-60 driver/docs do not yet surface that precondition early. This is a non-blocking harness usability follow-up, not a production scope-gate bypass.
- Unauthenticated dashboard retry paths and `checkLeadScope` behavior for missing identity remain outside FLY-127 and require a separate hardening change.
